03

The two rounds

Round 1 is paperwork you upload. Round 2 is everything else, on one morning at DPS R.K. Puram. The final result combines engineering, presentation and race scores.

R1 · Design & documentation

Online · due 22 Oct
DOC 01
Technical write-up

Max 30 pages. Explain why, not only what.

  • Design and aerodynamic reasoning
  • Materials, and how you made each part
  • Development process: what you tried and changed
  • Key decisions and what they cost
DOC 02
CAD package

Drawings detailed enough to check the car against.

  • 3-view drawing (top, side, front) with every dimension marked
  • Exploded view with a parts list
  • The CAD file itself, which you'll open for the jury on race day
DOC 03
Team identity

One page. Who you are as a constructor.

  • Constructor name and logo
  • Livery concept
  • Sponsorship plan, including what a sponsor gets back

R2 · Competition day

Onsite · 24 Oct · report 07:45 AM
  1. 1
    Scrutineering

    The car is weighed, put through a go/no-go box for length, width and height, and checked against the rest of the specs. Small problems can be fixed within 20 minutes. A car that still fails cannot race.

  2. 2
    Engineering evaluation

    Open your CAD model and walk the jury through it. Expect questions on why you made each choice.

  3. 3
    Team presentation

    Your pit-wall pitch: why the car looks the way it does, and why a sponsor should back you.

  4. 4
    Pit display

    A stand with your branding, sponsor material and team identity. Judges score it as part of branding and marketing.

  5. 5
    Timed runs

    The car runs along a guide line (tether) down a 20 m straight. Your best valid time counts.

Bring: the car, spares, your pit display, and laptops with your CAD files, charged.

    06

    Rules & contacts

    How to submit Round 1, how race day runs, and who to ask.

    Submitting Round 1

    1. Open the Submission Portal with your School UID.
    2. Upload all three documents: the technical write-up (max 30 pages), the CAD package (3-view drawing, exploded view with parts list) and the one-page team identity.
    3. Submit by 22 October. Check the portal for the exact cut-off time.
    4. Keep your Token of Submission. You need it to check in on 24 October.

    Conclave rules

    01Reporting

    Welcome Foyer, DPS R.K. Puram, 07:45–08:45 AM. Late arrivals forfeit preliminary heats. IDs are issued on arrival; bring your Token of Submission.

    02Cartridges

    Only event marshals handle and puncture CO2 cartridges. Teams never load their own.

    03Host school

    DPS R.K. Puram teams compete for benchmarking only. They can't take rankings, podium trophies or the Overall trophy.

    04Hardware

    Bring your own laptops, chargers and adapters, fully charged. Power outlets are provided; the host accepts no liability for personal hardware.

    05Judges' decisions

    Jury and marshal decisions are final. Disrespect towards arbiters or marshals means immediate disqualification.

    06Attire

    School uniform is fine. Any outfit appropriate for a school setting is allowed.

    Race-day itinerary
