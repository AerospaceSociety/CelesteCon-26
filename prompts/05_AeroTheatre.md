# Event 05: AEROSS Theatre (ATh)
**Discipline:** Open Stage & Theatrical Talent Showcase  
**Eligibility:** Senior Track (Grades 9–12)  
**Team Composition:** 3–5 members (Same school delegation)  
**School Limit:** Max 1 team per school  
**Competition Mode:** Hybrid (Online Video Audition → Onsite Live Showcase & Improv at Audio-Visual Hall)  
**Official Motto:** *"All the world’s a stage, and all the men and women merely players." — William Shakespeare*

---

## 1. Executive Summary
AEROSS Theatre is the conclave's flagship creative and dramatic arena. Delegations enjoy total artistic freedom: theatrical skits, stand-up comedy, monologues, musical ensemble, dance drama, mime, mimicry, or illusionism. No strict aerospace or STEM theme is mandated—pure theatrical brilliance, comic timing, and stagecraft govern the scoring.

---

## 2. Round Structure & Deliverables

### Round 1: Video Audition Tape
- **Submission Deadline:** 18 October 2026, 23:59:59 IST
- **Format:** 3–5 minute video recording uploaded to Google Drive or YouTube (Unlisted).
- **Guidelines:** Clear audio capture, stable framing, and natural acoustics. Performances must not exceed 5 minutes under any circumstance.
- **Shortlist:** Top delegations advance to perform live at the main Audio-Visual Hall (AVH).

### Round 2: Live Stage Showcase & Improvised Challenge
- **Date & Reporting:** 24 October 2026, 07:45 AM at Welcome Foyer, DPS R.K. Puram
- **Performance Stage:** Audio-Visual Hall (AVH) equipped with dynamic stage lighting, vocal collar mics, hand microphones, and stereo audio playback systems.
- **Part A (Showcase):** 6–8 minute live stage performance of the team's audition act (with enhancements).
- **Part B (Improv Challenge):** On-the-spot improvisational prompt given immediately following the act, challenging the troupe's quick thinking, spontaneity, and stage chemistry under 60-second prep time.

---

## 3. Evaluation Criteria (100 Points Total)
- **Creativity, Originality & Scriptwriting (30 pts):** Freshness of concept, dialogue wit, dramatic pacing, and thematic resolution.
- **Stage Presence, Expressiveness & Delivery (30 pts):** Voice modulation, blocking, body language, facial expressiveness, and emotional range.
- **Audience Engagement & Entertainment Value (20 pts):** Audience connection, comedic timing, musicality, and dramatic impact.
- **Improvisational Spontaneity (20 pts):** Presence of mind, collaborative improv coherence, and flair under surprise prompt conditions.
