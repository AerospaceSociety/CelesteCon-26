# Event 07: Model Rocketry & OpenRocket Simulation
**Discipline:** Aerospace Model Engineering & Flight Simulation  
**Eligibility:** Junior (Grades 6–8) · Senior (Grades 9–12) — Ranked Separately  
**Team Composition:** 2–3 members (Same school delegation)  
**Competition Mode:** Onsite (Digital Model Submission → Onsite Measurement & Standardized Flight Simulation)  
**Official Motor:** Estes D12-5 (24 × 70 mm casing from OpenRocket library)  
**Safety Mandate:** Zero Live Motors on Campus. Disqualification for any combustible material.

---

## 1. Executive Summary
Teams design, size, and construct a real model rocket around an Estes D12-5 motor mount, without ever fitting or bringing live propellant onto school grounds. On event day at DPS R.K. Puram, arbiters measure the physical rocket on a digital balance and with precision calipers, enter measured values into OpenRocket, and execute computerized flight simulations under identical atmospheric and rail conditions.

---

## 2. Motor Specification: Estes D12-5
- **Diameter:** 24 mm
- **Length:** 70 mm
- **Loaded Mass:** ≈ 44 g
- **Propellant Mass:** ≈ 21 g
- **Total Impulse:** ≈ 16.8 N·s
- **Average Thrust:** ≈ 10 N
- **Burn Time:** ≈ 1.7 s
- **Parachute Ejection Delay:** 5 s
- **Liftoff Mass Rating:** Up to 283 g max liftoff weight
- **Source:** Figures from ThrustCurve.org database in OpenRocket.

---

## 3. Mandatory Build Guidelines & Safety
- **Empty Motor Bay:** A 24 mm diameter motor tube at least 70 mm deep with a positive motor hook or retainer. Judges test fit with a dummy 24 × 70 mm casing.
- **Allowed Materials:** Balsa wood, basswood, Kraft paper tubing, cardstock, 3D printed components (PLA/PETG/Resin), fiberglass laminates, shock cords, and packed recovery parachute/streamer.
- **Prohibited:** Commercial ready-to-fly (RTF) kits, undeclared parts, metal nose cones/airframes, loose lead sinkers, or custom edited thrust curves.
- **ABSOLUTE BAN:** Zero motors, propellants, igniters, black powder, or pressurised canisters on campus. Violation leads to immediate delegation disqualification.

---

## 4. What Gets Scrutineered Onsite
1. **Empty Airframe Mass:** Determines wet liftoff mass and final apogee.
2. **Overall Length & Outer Diameter:** Dictates frontal drag area and the dimension of 1 caliber.
3. **Dry Center of Gravity (CG):** Measured by balancing empty airframe on knife-edge stand.
4. **Fin Geometry:** Root chord, tip chord, span, and sweep measured with digital calipers to compute Center of Pressure (CP).
5. **Surface Finish Quality:** Visual inspection matched to OpenRocket aerodynamic roughness categories.

---

## 5. Judging Criteria (100 Points Total)
- **Criterion A: Simulated Flight Performance:** Maximum altitude (apogee) and maintenance of static stability margin between 1.0 and 2.5 calibers throughout motor burn.
- **Criterion B: Digital-to-Physical Fidelity:** Precision match between the physical rocket on the scale and the uploaded `.ork` design file.
- **Criterion C: Build Quality & Craftsmanship:** Clean fin fillets, structural symmetry, rigid joints, and proper recovery system packing.
