# Event 01: Settle-Me-This (SMT)
**Discipline:** Space Settlement Design  
**Eligibility:** Junior (Grades 6–8) · Senior (Grades 9–12) — Judged Separately  
**Team Composition:** 3–5 members (Same school delegation)  
**School Limit:** Max 1 team per category (Junior / Senior)  
**Competition Mode:** Hybrid (Online Round 1 Proposal → Onsite Round 2 Defense at DPS R.K. Puram)  
**Official Motto:** *"Mankind was born on Earth. It was never meant to die here."*

---

## 1. Executive Summary
Settle-Me-This challenges student delegations to author an engineering proposal for a fully functioning, permanent orbital space settlement situated in free space (L5 Lagrangian point, Molniya orbit, or Earth-Moon halo orbit). The settlement must not be grounded upon a planetary surface or natural moon, though lunar/asteroidal resource harvesting and transport logistics may serve as supporting operations.

---

## 2. Round Structure & Deliverables

### Round 1: Online Engineering Proposal
- **Release Date:** Prompts unlock 13 October 2026, 00:00:00 IST
- **Submission Deadline:** 18 October 2026, 23:59:59 IST
- **Format:** Digital PDF Document Upload
  - **Junior Category (Grades 6–8):** Max 25 pages
  - **Senior Category (Grades 9–12):** Max 35 pages
- **Mandatory Sections:**
  1. Executive Summary & Mission Scope
  2. Structural Architecture, Geometry & Artificial Gravity (Centrifugal)
  3. Radiation Shielding & Environmental Control & Life Support Systems (ECLSS)
  4. Propulsion, Orbital Station-Keeping & Attitude Dynamics
  5. Power Generation (Solar, Nuclear, Fusion) & Thermal Management
  6. Socio-Economic Infrastructure, Community Planning, Governance & Ergonomics
  7. 2D/3D Schematics & Digital Renderings (Blender, Fusion 360, SketchUp)

### Round 2: Onsite Oral Defense & Exhibit (Top 5 per Category)
- **Date & Reporting:** 24 October 2026, 07:45 AM at Welcome Foyer, DPS R.K. Puram
- **Presentation Format:** 7-minute formal slide defense followed by 5 minutes of intensive jury cross-questioning.
- **Supporting Media:** Physical architectural models, 3D printed modules, or interactive simulations are highly encouraged.

---

## 3. Evaluation Criteria (100 Points Total)
- **Research & Scientific Rigor (25 pts):** Accuracy of orbital mechanics, atmospheric pressurization, and radiation shielding physics.
- **Technical Feasibility & Systems Integration (25 pts):** Closed-loop life support sustainability, power budget, and construction sequence.
- **Innovation & Creative Engineering (20 pts):** Novel architectural layouts, psychological well-being designs, and modular expansions.
- **Document Clarity & Visual Schematics (15 pts):** Standard technical drafting, clear diagrams, annotations, and citation integrity.
- **Jury Defense & Rebuttal (15 pts):** Cohesive team communication, poise, and technical mastery during cross-examination.
