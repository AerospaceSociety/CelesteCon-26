# Event 08: AEROSS Prix
**Discipline:** Miniature F1 Aerodynamic Racecar Engineering & Sprints  
**Eligibility:** Senior Track (Grades 9–12)  
**Team Composition:** 3–5 members (Same school delegation)  
**Competition Mode:** Onsite (Online Round 1 Engineering Dossier → Onsite Round 2 Scrutineering & 20m Track Sprints)  
**Propulsion:** Standard 12 g CO₂ cartridge (Supplied & handled exclusively by event marshals)  
**Budget Cap:** ₹30,000 Total Expenditure (Tooling, raw materials, spares, and paddock display)

---

## 1. Executive Summary
Teams found an F1 scale constructor, taking a miniature CO2-powered racing car from computational CAD modeling and aerodynamic airflow optimization through manufacturing, livery branding, pit display setup, and high-speed sprint races along a 20-meter straight track.

---

## 2. Technical Regulations & Dimensional Envelope
- **Overall Length:** 170 mm minimum – 210 mm maximum
- **Width Across Wheels:** ≤ 85 mm maximum
- **Overall Height:** ≤ 65 mm maximum
- **Minimum Dry Mass (without cartridge):** ≥ 50 g minimum
- **Wheel Diameters:** 26 mm minimum – 34 mm maximum (front and rear)
- **Minimum Wheel Width:** ≥ 15 mm minimum
- **Wheel Ground Contact:** All 4 wheels must make continuous, simultaneous contact with the racing surface.
- **Track Guide Tether:** Cars run along a taut guide wire. Chassis must include secure eyelets or lower guide slots.
- **CO2 Chamber:** Smooth, unobstructed cavity sized for standard 12 g gas cartridges. Cartridges are loaded and punctured strictly by race marshals.

---

## 3. Round Structure & Deliverables

### Round 1: Design Documentation & CAD Package (Due 22 Oct, Online)
1. **Technical Write-up (DOC 01):** Max 30 pages detailing aerodynamic reasoning, materials, fabrication techniques, and cost accounting.
2. **CAD Package (DOC 02):** Fully dimensioned 3-view orthographic drawing, exploded parts assembly view, and native 3D CAD files.
3. **Team Identity Portfolio (DOC 03):** Constructor name, logo emblem, livery rationale, and corporate sponsor return strategy.

### Round 2: Competition Day (24 Oct, Onsite DPS R.K. Puram)
1. **Technical Scrutineering:** Go/no-go box and scale check. Minor infractions may be fixed within a 20-minute pit window.
2. **Engineering Evaluation:** Interactive 3D CAD model defense before judges.
3. **Team Presentation:** 5-minute pit-wall presentation.
4. **Pit Display:** Static paddock booth showcasing constructor livery, sponsor branding, and engineering portfolio.
5. **Timed Sprint Races:** 20 m straight tethered sprint heats. Fastest valid run time counts.
