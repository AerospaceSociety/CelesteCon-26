# Event 04: Business Power Pitch (BPP)
**Discipline:** Aerospace Commercialization, Venture Capital & Pitch  
**Eligibility:** Junior (Grades 6–8) · Senior (Grades 9–12) — Judged Separately  
**Team Composition:** 3 members (Same school delegation)  
**School Limit:** Max 1 team per category (Junior / Senior)  
**Competition Mode:** Hybrid (Online Deck & Video Pitch → Onsite Shark-Tank Investor Defense)  
**Official Motto:** *"The most powerful person in the world is the storyteller." — Steve Jobs*

---

## 1. Executive Summary
Business Power Pitch challenges students to bridge cutting-edge deep-tech aerospace engineering with viable market economics. Teams invent a commercial space or aviation product/service (e.g. space debris remediation SaaS, suborbital point-to-point cargo, lunar in-situ resource mining, orbital tourism habitats, or urban air mobility logistics) and pitch for simulated seed funding.

---

## 2. Round Structure & Deliverables

### Round 1: Online Pitch Deck & Video Upload
- **Prompt Release:** 13 October 2026, 00:00:00 IST (Sub-track prompts published)
- **Submission Deadline:** 18 October 2026, 23:59:59 IST
- **Deliverables:**
  1. **Investor Pitch Deck (PDF):** 10–15 slides covering Problem Statement, Unique Value Proposition, Addressable Market (TAM/SAM/SOM), Unit Economics & Revenue Model, Go-To-Market Strategy, Technical Architecture, Competitor Matrix, and 5-Year Capital Allocation.
  2. **Elevator Pitch Video (MP4 / Google Drive Link):** 5-minute video pitch showcasing the venture in a dynamic, convincing manner.
- **Official Submission Template:** [Google Docs Template Link](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1wd8T4sqoSLX3euoxNoyVo6AzFL1JX9Vvt_xle5pmrMY/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.nmq48d4hymqb)

### Round 2: Onsite Live Investor Pitch & Challenger Interrogation
- **Date & Reporting:** 24 October 2026, 07:45 AM at DPS R.K. Puram
- **Presentation Slot:** 7 minutes of uninterrupted stage presentation followed by 5 minutes of intensive questioning by an investor panel.
- **Challenger Mechanism:** Competing teams seated in the audience may raise challenger questions to earn audience bonus points. Physical mockups, UI wireframes, and hardware prototypes are encouraged.

---

## 3. Evaluation Criteria (100 Points Total)
- **Commercial Viability & Unit Economics (25 pts):** Sensibility of pricing, operational margins, capital expenditures (CapEx), and cash runway.
- **Technical Soundness & Engineering Grounding (25 pts):** Grounded physics, realistic technology readiness level (TRL), and development roadmap.
- **Pitch Delivery & Storytelling (20 pts):** Stage presence, visual aesthetic of the slide deck, and persuasiveness.
- **Investor & Challenger Q&A Defense (15 pts):** Confidence under pressure, defensibility of financial projections, and strategic pivots.
- **Innovation & Market Disruption (15 pts):** Novelty of intellectual property and competitive moat against incumbents.
