# Event 02: Volatus
**Discipline:** Aviation, UAV & 3D CAD Modeling (incorporating Dimension III)  
**Eligibility:** Senior Track (Grades 9–12)  
**Team Composition:** 3 members (Same school delegation)  
**School Limit:** Max 1 team per school  
**Competition Mode:** Hybrid (Online Proposal + CAD Package → Onsite Science-Fair Exhibition & Q&A)  
**Official Motto:** *"Once you have tasted flight, you will forever walk the earth with your eyes turned skyward." — Leonardo da Vinci*

---

## 1. Executive Summary
Volatus merges aerodynamic sizing theory with digital manufacturing and mechanical packaging. Student design bureaus choose from one of three case-based mission profiles (e.g. disaster relief eVTOL, extreme-endurance wildfire surveillance UAV, or high-altitude Martian atmospheric exploration glider) and engineer an airframe from first principles.

---

## 2. Round Structure & Deliverables

### Round 1: Online Technical Proposal & 3D CAD
- **Prompt Release:** 13 October 2026, 00:00:00 IST (3 Mission Briefs Published)
- **Submission Deadline:** 18 October 2026, 23:59:59 IST
- **Deliverable Package:**
  1. **Engineering Proposal (PDF):** Max 20 pages detailing mission analysis, airfoil profile selection (NACA series), drag polar curves, center of gravity (CG) vs center of lift (CL) static margin, thrust-to-weight ratio, and avionics suite.
  2. **Digital 3D CAD Model:** Native files or standard export (`.step`, `.stl`, `.f3d`, or `.blend`) constructed in Autodesk Fusion 360, SolidWorks, Onshape, Blender, or FreeCAD.
  3. **3-View Orthographic Blueprint:** Top, side, and front elevations annotated with all millimeter dimensions and wing parameters.

### Round 2: Onsite Science-Fair Exhibition & Jury Defense
- **Date & Reporting:** 24 October 2026, 07:45 AM at Welcome Foyer, DPS R.K. Puram
- **Format:** Interactive science-fair booth setup. Judges, guest industry arbiters, and student delegations inspect exhibits continuously.
- **Physical Exhibits:** Teams are strongly encouraged to bring 3D-printed scale models, RC prototypes, or detailed structural cutaways alongside laptops running real-time CAD walkthroughs.

---

## 3. Evaluation Criteria (100 Points Total)
- **Aerodynamic Calculations & Sizing (25 pts):** Rigor of lift-to-drag estimation, wing loading, Reynolds number considerations, and powerplant selection.
- **CAD Detail & Geometric Accuracy (25 pts):** Assembly tree hierarchy, part tolerances, skin curvature continuity, and exploded assembly views.
- **Mission Profile Fit & Feasibility (20 pts):** Direct compliance with the selected case prompt requirements and operational payload constraints.
- **Craftsmanship of Physical Exhibit (15 pts):** Quality of display charts, physical models, prototypes, and booth presentation.
- **Defense & Arbiters Oral Q&A (15 pts):** Justification of aerodynamic trade-offs, structural materials, and rapid technical responses.
